Vision
What I'm building toward

The intersection of evidence-based psychology and contemplative tradition is one of the least-explored frontiers in digital mental health.

I'm interested in how clinical rigor and cultural depth can produce mental health tools that actually work — not just for majority populations, but for underserved ones. My research training, Jewish studies, and clinical exposure point toward joining or building a team working at the frontier of measurement-based, culturally-grounded digital therapeutics. Israel is where that conversation is happening at the highest level right now.
